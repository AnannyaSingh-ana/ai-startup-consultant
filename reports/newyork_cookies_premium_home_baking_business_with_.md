## Legal
**Considerations:**
- Business Registration: You'll need to register your business. In New York, this might mean forming an LLC or another suitable business structure, which can protect your personal assets.
- Food Business Licensing: Since this is a food business, you'll likely need a food license through New York's Department of Health or equivalent local entity to operate legally.
- Home Kitchen Regulations: If you plan to bake from home, check whether New York's Cottage Food Law allows for your product to be made in a home kitchen, and ensure compliance with all applicable regulations.
- Tax Compliance: Understand your tax obligations, which include sales tax registration and potentially income tax, depending on your chosen business structure.
- Food Safety: Adhering to food safety standards is crucial. This includes proper labeling of any allergens, as well as safe storage and transportation practices.
- Intellectual Property: Consider protecting your business name, logo, or unique recipes with trademarks to safeguard your brand.
- Insurance: Look into getting liability insurance to protect against potential claims, especially since you’re selling consumable products.
- Social Media Marketing Compliance: Ensure compliance with FTC guidelines for advertising and endorsements on Instagram, particularly if employing influencers.
- Privacy Considerations: If you collect customer data through your Instagram or website, adhere to privacy laws like CCPA if applicable, even though your primary focus is on Instagram.
- Packaging Regulations: Pay attention to any state-specific packaging requirements, including environmental regulations if applicable.

*This is not legal advice. Consult a licensed attorney for guidance specific to your business.*

## Founder Recommendation
**Launch?** NOT YET

**Reason:** While there is a healthy interest in premium home baking post-COVID and opportunities to capitalize on Instagram marketing, there is no specific demand signal or strong market validation for New York-style cookies in this channel. The competitive landscape is crowded with established players, and your unique selling proposition is not sufficiently differentiated. Further validation of demand is necessary before launching.

**Biggest Risk:** The biggest risk is market saturation and the challenge of converting Instagram engagement into actual sales, considering the lack of explicit demand for New York cookies specifically marketed on Instagram.

**MVP Suggestion:** Test the market with a limited product release, perhaps through local pop-up events or collaborations with local cafes, combined with an Instagram teaser campaign to gather direct customer feedback and refine your offering.

**First 100 Customers:** Leverage partnerships with local influencers and food bloggers to drive initial interest. Offer exclusive pre-orders or promotions for followers who engage with your content on Instagram.

**Next 30 Days:**
- Conduct a small-scale test of the product through local pop-ups or partnership cafés to validate interest and gather direct feedback.
- Increase Instagram activity, focusing on visually engaging content and building a community around premium home baking lifestyle to measure engagement levels.
- Identify and collaborate with at least three local influencers or food bloggers to evaluate the potential impact on brand visibility and customer acquisition.
